05About Me

I'm Yazonii. I like making ideas real.

I'm a young developer and creator who likes building things instead of just talking about them. My main world is the web, but I also mess around with apps, software, Minecraft projects, branding, and whatever new idea catches my attention.

I care a lot about how something feels. A website can work perfectly and still feel boring, generic, or like it came from the same template as everyone else. I want my work to have a point of view — clean when it needs to be clean, animated when animation actually adds something, and always recognisably mine.

I'm still learning. Some projects work, some get rebuilt three times, and some never make it past the idea stage. That's part of it. I like experimenting, breaking things, fixing them, and getting better with every version.

What I'm building right now +

I'm putting most of my energy into Yazonii: improving my web design skills, building a stronger portfolio, experimenting with products, and figuring out what kind of digital work I want to be known for.

What I'm into outside websites +

Minecraft, content creation, software ideas, aviation, design, and anything where I can build or learn something. I like projects that start as a random thought and turn into something people can actually use.

How I actually work +

I usually start by messing with the idea until I understand what feels right. Then I simplify it, build the important parts, test it, notice what looks bad, and keep refining. I'm not interested in making something look impressive for five seconds if the actual experience is weak.

A little more about me +

I'm based in Sweden, I work mainly from whatever setup I have available, and I'm constantly teaching myself new things. Yazonii is meant to grow with me — not pretend I'm already some huge studio.

I care about clear design, useful features, personality, and building from scratch.
