Hey, I’m Yazonii.

I’m a young builder from Sweden who is interested in coding, technology, Minecraft, design, and creating things that feel like they belong to me. I enjoy starting with a simple idea and seeing how far I can take it.

Sometimes that means working on a website. Sometimes it means experimenting with an app, a server, a game concept, or an idea that came to me randomly. I’m still learning, but I like learning by actually making things instead of just watching tutorials forever.

This portfolio is a snapshot of that process. It is not meant to pretend that I have already built everything. It is here to document the things I finish, the things I improve, and the projects I’m working toward. There is a lot more I want to create, and this is only the beginning.
